Play Point Gomoku online free: a fan-made strategy game inspired by The Devil’s Plan, with secret bidding, exactly-five wins, four AI levels and friend matches.

Select three consecutive empty cells in a straight line. Gain 10 points each round; bid at least 1 per cell. Ties favor the non-selector. Both bids are spent; half the remainder carries over. Exactly five wins; overlines do not count.

Unofficial fan-made game; not affiliated with the show.
