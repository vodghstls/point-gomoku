Rules

Two players, Black and White, play on a 15×15 board. Black selects first. Each round both receive 10 points plus half their previous unspent points, rounded down.

The selector has 90 seconds to choose three consecutive empty cells in a straight horizontal, vertical or diagonal line. Both players secretly bid at least one point per cell within their budget. Higher bids win each cell; ties favor the non-selector. Both players pay all bids. All three stones are placed before checking victory.

Only a maximal line of exactly five stones counts. Six or more is an overline and does not count. If both players form valid lines, the player with more wins; equal counts draw. No legal selection is a draw. Selection alternates each round.

Unofficial fan-made game; not affiliated with the show.
