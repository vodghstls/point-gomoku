Strategy guide

Think in triples

Evaluate all eight possible ownership patterns before bidding. Winning every cell can create an invalid overline.

Use tie priority

The non-selector wins equal bids. Allocate points accordingly, without assuming you know the opponent’s budget.

Balance attack and defense

Look for open fours, simultaneous threats and defensive blocks. Save points when a cell has little tactical value; half the remainder carries over.
